Privacy Notice

Interim version for early access — 2026-10-18-security-rsf

Who We Are

TimeTreasure is provided by Raja Khatib, as an individual, trading as Future Forward Creations ("we"). Contact: futureforwardcreations@gmail.com.

This notice explains what TimeTreasure records during the early-access test, why, and who can see it.

The employer decides which workers to add and which rates and amounts to use. We run the app and store the records.

What We Record

• Employers: name, email, household or business name, country, rates, monthly amounts and payroll records.

• Workers: name, the phone number or email the employer entered, clock-in and clock-out times, missed-shift requests, and the amounts calculated from the employer's settings.

If you add a profile photo, we store it privately and show it to your current and past employers in TimeTreasure. You can remove it at any time. It is deleted before your account is deleted.

• Tap location: only if the worker allows it (see "Location").

• Notification token: a code that lets the app send notifications to your phone. It may be stored. No notifications are sent yet.

Location

When a worker taps Clock In or Clock Out, and only if the worker allows it, TimeTreasure saves one position and its street address at that moment.

It never follows the worker during the shift. Clock-in works without location. The employer then sees "no location".

Location is used only to show the employer and the worker where each tap happened. It is never used to calculate pay, never to block clock-in, and never for marketing or profiling.

How We Use Data

Only to run TimeTreasure: sign-in, recording shifts, calculating amounts from the employer's settings, and showing the records to the employer and the worker.

No analytics. No ads. We never sell your data.

Who Can See It

Each record is shown only to the employer and that worker. We do not share it with anyone else, except the service providers below.

Service Providers and Countries

• Supabase: database and sign-in. Data is stored in Frankfurt, Germany. Supabase Pte. Ltd. is based in Singapore and has staff in other countries.

• Expo (USA): app updates.

• Apple or Google: your phone's map service turns the tap position into an address. "Open in Maps" opens the position in Google Maps.

• Twilio (USA): sign-in codes by SMS, when SMS sign-in is switched on.

• Cloudflare Turnstile (USA): a bot check, only when enabled. It receives network and device information, not your shift or pay records.

• Sentry (USA): crash reports, only when enabled. Reports exclude names, email addresses, phone numbers and locations.

How Long We Keep Data

Records are kept while the employer's account exists.

For closed shifts, tap coordinates, accuracy and addresses are cleared by a daily cleanup after 90 days from the last clock tap. Locations on an open shift are kept until it closes. Shift times and amounts are kept.

Deleting Your Account

Employers and workers can delete their account in Settings. This cannot be undone.

After deletion, we keep only a protected code derived from each verified email address or mobile number. We do not keep the contact itself, the name or a link to the deleted account in this record. This prevents a reused contact from taking over someone else’s history.

When a worker deletes their account, the employer keeps that worker's shift records (times, amounts and tap locations) as the employer's work records.

When an employer deletes their account, workers keep their own shift and payment history. The employer's account details are removed from that history.

Your Choices and Rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Email futureforwardcreations@gmail.com. We answer within 10 days.

You can stop sharing location at any time: turn off ‘Location at Clock In and Out’ in the app's Settings, or turn off location for TimeTreasure in your phone's settings. Clock-in still works.

Workers: if a shift record looks wrong, also tell your employer.

Age

TimeTreasure is only for people aged 18 or older.

Changes

We announce changes to this notice in the app. Employers must accept important changes again before they continue.
