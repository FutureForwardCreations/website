What TimeTreasure Does and Its Limits

TimeTreasure is a record-keeping and calculation tool. It records the times workers clock in and out (and, where the worker allows it, the location of each tap). It calculates amounts using only the rates, monthly amounts and other settings the employer enters.

TimeTreasure does not employ, recruit or place any worker. It is not a party to any employment or service relationship between an employer and a worker. It does not pay, hold or transfer money. Marking a month as "Paid" is the employer's own record. It is not proof that a payment was made.

TimeTreasure does not give legal, tax, accounting, payroll or employment advice. It does not check whether settings or payments comply with the law or any contract. Amounts shown may not include items the law or the contract requires. Examples: overtime premiums, rest-day or holiday pay, leave pay, end-of-service benefits, social-security contributions (such as NSSF in Lebanon) and deductions.

How Totals Work

For salaried workers, time outside extra-pay rates is covered by the monthly salary and shown as “In Salary”. Hourly time without an applicable rate is shown as “Unrated” and adds no hourly amount. Per-visit pay uses the amount set for each counted visit.

A shift paid by the hour or covered by a salary that lasts longer than 48 hours is shown without an amount. Per-visit pay counts one amount for each Clock In, however long the shift lasts. Its hours remain visible. Check incorrect times through the shift review; an entered time counts after employer approval.

A missed-shift request counts after employer approval. A shift approved after its month was marked paid is counted in the current open month, using its real date. Its amount is added on top of that month’s salary or minimum. The paid month stays unchanged.

Deleting Your Account

Employers and workers can delete their account in Settings. This cannot be undone.

After deletion, we keep only a protected code derived from each verified email address or mobile number. We do not keep the contact itself, the name or a link to the deleted account in this record. This prevents a reused contact from taking over someone else’s history.

When a worker deletes their account, the employer keeps that worker's shift records (times, amounts and tap locations) as the employer's work records.

When an employer deletes their account, workers keep their own shift and payment history. The employer's account details are removed from that history.
