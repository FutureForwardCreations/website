TimeTreasure

Fair, simple time tracking for the people who work for you.

Workers clock in and out with one tap. Pay is calculated from the rates the employer sets: by the hour, per visit or as a monthly salary.

Built for households and small businesses in the UAE and Lebanon, in English and Arabic.

TimeTreasure is in early-access testing. It is not yet available in the App Store or Google Play.
